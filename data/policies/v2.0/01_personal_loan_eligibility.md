# DEMO BANK - SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT - FOR EDUCATIONAL USE ONLY

**DemoBank is a fictional institution. Every threshold, product name and clause
in this document was invented for an educational capstone project. Nothing here
reflects the policy of any real bank and it must not be used to make or inform
a real lending decision.**

## PL-1.1 Purpose and scope

This policy sets out the pre-qualification criteria for the DemoBank Unsecured
Personal Loan. It applies to all pre-qualification enquiries handled by branch
staff, the contact centre, or an automated assistant. Pre-qualification is an
indicative assessment only: it does not create an offer of credit and it does
not commit DemoBank to lend.

Criteria are grouped as either **hard criteria**, which must be satisfied for a
pre-qualification to proceed, or **supporting criteria**, whose failure results
in a referral to a credit officer rather than an outright decline.

## PL-1.2 Policy version control

Version 2.0 is effective from 1 July 2025 and supersedes version 1.0. Enquiries
dated on or before 30 June 2025 must continue to be assessed under version 1.0.
Version 2.0 tightens affordability criteria and introduces a savings buffer
supporting criterion.

## PL-1.3 Summary of changes from version 1.0

- Minimum employment tenure raised from 6 months to **12 months** (PL-3.2).
- Maximum loan amount raised from 50,000 to **60,000** currency units (PL-4.1).
- Income multiple cap reduced from 5.0x to **4.5x** (PL-4.2).
- New savings buffer supporting criterion (PL-5.2).
- Minimum credit score raised from 640 to 660 and maximum debt-to-income
  reduced from 45% to 40% by the Credit and Affordability Standards v2.0
  (CR-2.1, CR-3.1).
- New stressed affordability hard criterion (CR-3.3).

## PL-2.1 Applicant age

The applicant must be at least **21 years of age** and no more than **70 years
of age** at the date of application.

## PL-2.2 Residency status

The applicant must hold one of the following residency statuses:

- Citizen
- Permanent resident
- Long-term visa holder (residual visa term of at least 24 months)

Short-term visa holders and non-residents are not eligible.

## PL-3.1 Minimum income

Gross verifiable annual income must be at least **24,000 currency units**.

## PL-3.2 Employment stability

The applicant must be in full time, part time, self employed or contract work
and must have been in the current employment or trading arrangement for at
least **12 consecutive months**. This tenure requirement was raised from 6
months in version 1.0 following portfolio review.

## PL-3.4 Additional self-employment condition

Where the applicant is self employed, a trading history of at least **24
months** is expected. A shorter trading history is a supporting criterion
failure and results in referral to a credit officer.

## PL-4.1 Loan amount limits

The Unsecured Personal Loan is offered between **2,000 and 60,000 currency
units**.

## PL-4.2 Income multiple cap

The requested loan amount must not exceed **4.5 times** gross verifiable annual
income.

## PL-4.3 Payment assumptions used in assessment

Indicative monthly repayments are calculated on a standard amortising basis
using:

- Representative annual interest rate: **12.90%**
- Default assessment term: **60 months**

## PL-5.1 Outcome categories

Pre-qualification produces exactly one of four outcomes:

1. **Eligible** - all hard and supporting criteria are satisfied.
2. **Eligible with conditions** - all hard criteria are satisfied but one or
   more supporting criteria require verification or credit officer review.
3. **Not eligible** - at least one hard criterion is failed.
4. **Insufficient information** - a hard criterion cannot be evaluated because
   required applicant data is missing.

An outcome of *insufficient information* must never be communicated as a
decline.

## PL-5.2 Savings buffer

The applicant is expected to hold liquid savings of at least **three times**
total monthly credit obligations, including the indicative repayment on the
requested facility. A shortfall, or an undeclared savings position, is a
supporting criterion failure and results in referral, not a decline.
