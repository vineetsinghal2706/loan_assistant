# DEMO BANK - SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT - FOR EDUCATIONAL USE ONLY

**Synthetic content created for an educational capstone project. The scores,
ratios and waiting periods below are invented.**

## CR-1.1 Purpose

These standards apply across all DemoBank retail lending products and are read
together with the product eligibility policies. Where a product policy and
these standards both address a criterion, the stricter of the two applies.

## CR-1.3 Summary of changes from version 1.0

- Minimum credit scores raised for all three products (CR-2.1).
- Maximum debt-to-income ratios reduced for all three products (CR-3.1).
- New stressed affordability hard criterion introduced (CR-3.3).
- Bankruptcy discharge waiting period reduced from 5 years to **4 years**
  (CR-4.1).

## CR-2.1 Minimum credit score by product

The DemoBank internal credit score runs from 300 to 900. Minimum scores for
pre-qualification under version 2.0 are:

| Product | Minimum score (v2.0) | Previous (v1.0) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unsecured Personal Loan | **660** | 640 |
| Residential Mortgage | **680** | 660 |
| Auto Loan | **640** | 620 |

A score below the product minimum is a hard criterion failure.

## CR-2.2 Applicants with no score

Where no internal credit score can be produced, the enquiry must be recorded as
*insufficient information* and the applicant invited to complete a full
application so that a score can be generated. It must not be recorded as a
decline.

## CR-2.3 Credit history depth

A credit history of at least **24 months** is expected. A shorter history is a
supporting criterion failure, producing referral for thin-file assessment
rather than a decline.

## CR-3.1 Maximum debt-to-income ratio

The debt-to-income ratio is calculated as total monthly credit obligations,
including the indicative repayment on the requested facility, divided by gross
monthly income. Maximum ratios under version 2.0 are:

| Product | Maximum DTI (v2.0) | Previous (v1.0) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unsecured Personal Loan | **40%** | 45% |
| Residential Mortgage | **40%** | 43% |
| Auto Loan | **42%** | 45% |

## CR-3.2 Gross monthly income

Gross monthly income is gross verifiable annual income, including documented
secondary income, divided by twelve.

## CR-3.3 Stressed affordability

In addition to the test in CR-3.1, the debt-to-income ratio recalculated with
the representative interest rate increased by **2.00 percentage points** must
not exceed **50%**. This stressed test is a hard criterion and applies to the
Unsecured Personal Loan and the Residential Mortgage.

## CR-4.1 Bankruptcy and insolvency

An applicant with a prior bankruptcy or formal insolvency arrangement may be
pre-qualified only where the arrangement was discharged at least **4 years**
before the date of enquiry. An undischarged arrangement, or discharge within
the last 4 years, is a hard criterion failure.

## CR-4.2 Active defaults

The applicant should have **no active defaults** recorded. One or more active
defaults is a supporting criterion failure and results in referral to a credit
officer.

## CR-5.1 Maximum lending capacity

Indicative maximum lending capacity is the lowest of:

1. the product maximum amount,
2. the product income multiple applied to gross verifiable annual income, and
3. the principal whose indicative repayment, added to existing monthly credit
   obligations, exactly reaches the product maximum debt-to-income ratio.

Capacity figures are rounded **down** to the nearest 100 currency units and are
indicative only.
