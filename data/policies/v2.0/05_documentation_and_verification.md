# DEMO BANK - SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT - FOR EDUCATIONAL USE ONLY

**Synthetic content created for an educational capstone project.**

## DOC-1.1 Purpose

This document lists the evidence DemoBank expects to see before a
pre-qualification outcome can be relied upon.

## DOC-2.1 Verification status at pre-qualification

Where supplied income, employment and obligation data have not yet been
verified against documents, the outcome must be issued as *eligible with
conditions* and the outstanding verification listed. Unverified data is a
supporting criterion failure, never a hard failure.

## DOC-2.2 Standard evidence set

- Photographic identity document
- Proof of current address dated within the last 3 months
- Three most recent payslips, or two years of trading accounts for
  self-employed applicants
- Three most recent current-account statements
- Evidence of liquid savings, where a savings buffer or reserves criterion
  applies (PL-5.2, MG-6.2)
- For mortgage applications, evidence of deposit funds

## DOC-3.1 Self-employed applicants

Self-employed applicants must provide two years of trading accounts or tax
computations prepared by a qualified accountant. Where only one year is
available, the enquiry is referred for credit officer review under section
PL-3.4.

## DOC-4.1 Data minimisation

Only the evidence listed in DOC-2.2 may be collected at pre-qualification.
Automated assistants must not request or store identity document numbers,
account numbers, or any other sensitive identifier, and must record applicant
data under a pseudonymous reference.

## DOC-5.1 Explainability and record keeping

Every pre-qualification outcome must be recorded with: the policy version
applied, the identifier of each criterion evaluated, the outcome of each
criterion, the data values relied upon, and the policy clauses cited to the
applicant. The record must be reproducible: re-running the same applicant data
against the same policy version must produce the same outcome.

## DOC-5.2 Role of automated explanation

An automated assistant may generate the natural-language explanation of an
outcome, but the outcome itself must be produced by the deterministic
assessment of the criteria in the applicable policy version. The assistant must
not restate, qualify or reverse the recorded outcome, and every criterion it
mentions must be supported by a citation to the policy version applied.
